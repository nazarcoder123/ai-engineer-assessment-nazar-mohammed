Title: Athena, Goddess of Wisdom and Strategic Warfare
Category: Greek Deities & Legends
Keywords: Athena, Minerva, wisdom, warfare, strategy, Parthenon, Athens, Metis, owl, olive tree

Athena (or Pallas Athena) is the ancient Greek goddess of wisdom, warfare, strategy, intellect, justice, mathematics, and handicrafts. Unlike Ares, who revels in mindless slaughter and physical frenzy, Athena represents disciplined tactical acumen, civic defence, and heroic cunning.

Miraculous Birth:
Zeus swallowed his first wife, the Titaness Metis (wisdom), after a prophecy warned that her child would surpass him. Later, Zeus was struck by an agonizing headache. The divine blacksmith Hephaestus split Zeus's skull open with an adze at the river Triton, whereupon Athena leapt forth in full adult splendour, clad in gleaming bronze armor, shouting a war cry.

Patron of Athens and Heroes:
- Patronage of Athens: Athena competed against Poseidon to become the patron deity of the city of Athens. Poseidon struck the Acropolis with his trident, creating a saltwater spring; Athena planted an olive tree, symbolizing peace, prosperity, and nourishment. The citizens chose Athena, and built the Parthenon in her honor.
- Protector of Heroes: Athena served as divine mentor and protector to Greece's greatest champions, including Perseus (giving him a mirrored shield to slay Medusa), Heracles (aiding in his labours), and Odysseus during his ten-year voyage in Homer's Odyssey.
- Symbols: The little owl (Athene noctua, representing insight into darkness), the olive tree, and the Aegis bearing the head of Medusa.
